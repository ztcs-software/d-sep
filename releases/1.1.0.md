Fixes for moves to another drive, Undo and verifying, plus a few smaller ones

- F5 and Ctrl+R no longer reload the window, which made it lose track of a sort still running, along with its Cancel button and its Undo
- A **Save to** folder inside the scanned folder is now recognized even when its path is spelled with different capitals, so sorting again leaves the clips already there in place
- **Verify copies** is no longer skipped on a move to another drive started before D-Sep had checked which drive the **Save to** folder is on
- When a move to another drive can't remove an original (from a locked card, say), the copy now counts in the result, and the clip's `.LRF` and `.SRT` files are copied along with it
- Undo no longer gets stuck on a clip it moved back from another drive but couldn't remove from where it was sorted to
- When an `.MP4` and a `.MOV` share a name, their `.LRF` and `.SRT` files now go with the `.MP4`
- Progress now says **Verifying** while a clip's `.LRF` and `.SRT` files are read back too, not only the clip
- **Choose another folder…** and dropping a folder on the window now warn that Undo won't be offered after that, as **Sort again** does
- Sizes just under a unit now round up the way Explorer shows them: 1.0 KB, not 1024 B
