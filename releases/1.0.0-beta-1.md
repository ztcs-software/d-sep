The first release of D-Sep, as a beta. It finds the DJI clips shot in D-Log, D-Log 2 or D-Log M and separates
them from the rest of your footage.

- Sorts log clips into `D-Log`, `D-Log 2` and `D-Log M` folders, adds a prefix to their names (`DL_`, `DL2_`,
  `DLM_` or your own), or both.
- Copies or moves them, in place or into another folder, with their `.LRF` and `.SRT` files. Nothing is ever
  overwritten, and a move can be undone.
- Clips that are already sorted are marked in the list before you start.
- **Verify copies** reads each copy back and compares it with the original.
- Light and dark themes.
- Comes as an installer, or as a portable `.exe` that offers to sort the folder it's in.
